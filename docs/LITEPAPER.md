# EELP Terminal: Technical Litepaper

## Crypto Market Intelligence & On-Chain Forensics Platform

---

### 1. Abstract
EELP Terminal is a decentralized market intelligence and on-chain forensic platform engineered to reveal what is happening beneath raw price charts. By analyzing contract bytecode privileges, wallet relationship graphs, liquidity pool depth, and flow divergence, EELP translates complex blockchain traces into structured, understandable market intelligence.

---

### 2. The Problem
Traditional crypto dashboards only answer superficial questions:
* *What is the price?*
* *What is the 24H volume?*
* *How many wallets hold the token?*

These metrics fail to detect coordinated cluster accumulation, liquidity migration vulnerabilities, or transfer tax exploits.

---

### 3. The EELP Solution (Crypto X-Ray)
EELP answers critical forensic questions:
* **WHY** is the market behaving this way?
* **WHO** is driving the volume (retail vs synchronized clusters)?
* **HOW** concentrated is ownership among top entities?
* **WHERE** is capital moving (CEX, DEX, or cold storage)?
* **IS** liquidity depth expanding proportionally to market capitalization?
